# Personal blog visual direction

Scope: the shared Hexo blog shell and its homepage, navigation, post listings, articles, archives, tags, and About page. Visitor mode: Read. Visitors come to find and read personal posts; the existing routes, content behavior, responsive layout, and accessible navigation remain intact. The direction is explicitly pinned by the user: a muted off-white/light-gray palette and a simple overlapping four-square composition. No biography or post content will be invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet editorial blog whose navigation is a four-part geometric composition, not a row of generic pills or cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white grounds, layered light-gray square planes, graphite text, crisp edges, and a single cluster of four overlapping square navigation tiles; reading content remains a calm, high-contrast column.

STORY: Readers recognize the site, choose Home, About, Archive, or Tags, and move directly into familiar post lists and articles.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact masthead places the site name opposite a two-by-two cluster of four square links. Their muted tones distinguish the tiles without carrying meaning by color alone. A plain page heading and single-column post list follow beneath on an off-white reading surface.
